# Contributing

Pull requests are always welcome: variations, additions, removals, other tunings, other endings, a sixth axiom, a bug fix, a better pluck, or anything else. There's no roadmap to fit into and no style police. If it makes the piece more interesting, or just differently interesting, send it.

Issues aren't only for problems. Open one just to say hi, ask how something works, share what you made with it, argue about whether B♯ really is C, or have a friendly chat about tuning theory. No bug required.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution is shared under the same [Comma License](LICENSE.md).
